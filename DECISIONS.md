# Decisions

Architecture decision records for `airline-delays`. One entry per decision, short, dated. Superseded entries are kept and marked. Portuguese summaries of the evidence live in the research notes referenced below; the decision text is in English because this file is part of the public repository.

Evidence base: `docs/notes/` (Portuguese) and the reconstruction reports produced before the repository existed (`reconstrucao-vra.md`, `avaliacao-vra-como-fonte.md`, `revisao-plano-sota.md`, `fontes-vra-anac.md`, `referencias-replicacao-ml.md`, `tabela3-resultados.md`).

## ADR-0000 — VRA licence and redistribution (2026-09-05)

**Context.** ANAC's website footer states CC BY-ND 3.0 for "all content of this site"; no ANAC page declares a licence for the dataset itself. Read literally, ND forbids derivatives.

**Evidence.** The federal open-data catalogue declares `Licença: Creative Commons Attribution` for the dataset "Voos e operações aéreas - Voo Regular Ativo (VRA)" (https://dados.gov.br/dados/conjuntos-dados/dadosabertos-areas-de-atuacao-voos-e-operacoes-aereas-voo-regular-ativo-vra, read 2026-09-05; catalogued 2019-03-01, metadata updated 2024-01-25, responsible unit GOPE). Licence version is not stated.

**Decision.** Treat the VRA as CC BY. Attribute as "ANAC, Voo Regular Ativo (VRA), via dados.gov.br". Redistribute raw snapshots and derived data with attribution. File an e-SIC request for written confirmation in parallel; do not block on it. Record the catalogue page and read date in `README.md` and `datapackage.json`.

## ADR-0001 — Nodes: Viracopos inside São Paulo (2026-09-05)

**Evidence.** With Viracopos (SBKP) in the São Paulo node, exact agreement of `f` with the private benchmark panel rises from 86.8% to 97.4%. The tariff base labels Viracopos as "Campinas", but the panel's `f` equals the tariff base's own metropolitan count.

**Decision.** Nodes are the 24 single-airport capitals plus MRSP = SBSP + SBGR + SBKP, MRRJ = SBGL + SBRJ, MRBH = SBBH + SBCF. Every table carries `o_icao` and `d_icao` next to `o` and `d`, so airport-level work never requires re-extraction.

## ADR-0002 — Universe (2026-09-05)

**Evidence.** Regressing the panel's `f` on flight counts by category gives β ≈ 1 for line types N/R/E with DI 0 and ≈ 0 elsewhere; admitting DI ∈ {0,1,2} drops agreement from 97% to 54%; `f` includes cancelled flights (`prcanc = fl_can / f`).

**Decision.** Replication universe: line types N, R, E; DI 0; realised and cancelled flights. Prediction universe: realised flights of the same universe; extras and return flights become features, never filters. Column names carry the universe; a test forbids silent mixing. Known limitation: departure-delay counts reproduce at 92.4% and arrival-delay counts at 59.8% under the same rule; the asymmetry is declared, not resolved.

## ADR-0003 — Airline groups, mergers and classes (2026-09-05)

**Decision.** `data/external/groups.csv` is a dated table: `airline, group, start, end, class, source, url`. Initial content follows the laboratory rule that reproduces the FSC proportions with zero median difference: Varig group (VRG, VLO, VRN, NES, RSL) becomes Gol from 2007-04; Webjet becomes Gol from 2011-11; Pantanal becomes TAM from 2009-12; TTL becomes Trip from 2007-11; Trip becomes Azul from 2012-05. Classes: FSC = TAM, Varig, Vasp, Transbrasil; LCC = Gol, Azul, Webjet; regional = Trip, Passaredo, Pantanal and the rest. Open cases decided: Varig after 2007-04 counts as Gol (LCC); Webjet 2005–2011 is an independent LCC; Avianca Brasil (Oceanair) is FSC. Every row cites a source (CADE decision, ANAC act, or press of record) with a confidence grade.

## ADR-0004 — Grain and storage format (2026-09-05)

**Evidence.** At daily grain 81% of route-day cells have fewer than 10 flights and a 22% prevalence has a standard error of 0.145; weekly 18% and 0.055; monthly 2.6% and 0.027. The full flight table is 250–290 MB in partitioned parquet.

**Decision.** The record grain is the flight. The canonical fact table is `group × route × month`. Route-month, city-month and airline-city-month tables are generated by `aggregate()` and tested for additivity; order statistics are recomputed from flights at each grain. Weekly and daily aggregates exist on demand only and are never committed. Storage: parquet, zstd level 9, tight types, partitioned by year; canonical above 2 MB, `csv.gz` below; the replication panel ships in both. Flight tables go to Zenodo, not to git. No DVC; no `.duckdb` files in git.

## ADR-0005 — Delay-cause code taxonomy (2026-09-05)

**Evidence.** The article's `prwheather`, reproduced at 98.5%, merges weather with closed or restricted airports, and its dominant code is AR ("aeroporto com restrições operacionais"). `princident` = DF, DG, HB, MA, TD (99.1%); `pr_connc` = RA, "aircraft connection", which the article describes as waiting for connecting passengers (99.2%).

**Decision.** Keep the article's three columns exactly. Add a second taxonomy alongside: weather (WO, WT, WA, WR, WS, WI, AM; cancellations XO, XT, XS); airport closed or restricted (AI, AJ, AR; XI, XJ, XM); aircraft rotation (RA, RI, RM); technical (TD, DF, DG, MA, HB); operational and air traffic (AT, FP, GF, AS, AF, AG); authorised (HA, HD, OA, XA, XB); other. The AR finding is reported under "Declared differences" in the README.

## ADR-0007 — Congestion (2026-09-05)

**Evidence.** The BNDES/McKinsey 2010 study reports airport capacity in passengers per year, not movements per hour; hourly declared capacity lives in ANAC's seasonal capacity declarations, not yet collected. An internal proxy (day-hours above the city's own 90th percentile within the year) already exists.

**Decision.** Ship both: the internal p90 proxy now, and the declared-capacity definition when the ANAC declarations are collected. The article's `prcongested` is marked as not reproduced until then.

## ADR-0008 — Outlier rule and sign of delays (2026-09-05)

**Evidence.** The laboratory used 313.25 minutes in one script and 117.10 (arrival) with 111.75 (departure) in another; the panel does not trim `f`; FSC minutes keep early arrivals negative and remove outliers by an unidentified rule.

**Decision.** Outlier threshold is a named parameter with default 313.25 minutes; early arrivals stay negative; the replication report carries a sensitivity table of the main coefficient across thresholds.

## ADR-0009 — Evaluation design for delay prediction (2026-09-05)

**Evidence.** 43.6% of arrivals were late in 2007 and 15.7% in 2013; P(late | previous leg late) = 0.79 against 0.11; the literature's most common error is a random split.

**Decision.** Rolling-origin evaluation 2006–2013 is the headline result; the fixed split 2002–2010 / 2011 / 2012–2013 is illustrative. Two horizons reported separately: day-ahead (D−1) and at-gate (H−1, with the previous leg's actual delay). Metrics: AUC, PR-AUC, Brier, calibration. Leakage tests are part of the test suite: nothing from the flight after departure, no same-period aggregate containing the flight, historical windows closed before the flight date.

## ADR-0010 — Governance of divergences (2026-09-05)

**Decision.** When two implementation options diverge and the evidence does not settle it, a panel of independent reviewers evaluates both and the recommended option is adopted; the divergence and the panel's reasoning are recorded here.
