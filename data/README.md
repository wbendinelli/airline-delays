# data/

The data of `airline-delays`, layer by layer: what each directory holds, what
is tracked in git, what is regenerated from ANAC's files, and which of the two
panels to use for what. `docs/dictionary.md` defines every column and
`datapackage.json` describes every tracked table as a Frictionless Data
Package; this page is the guide to both.

## The layers

```text
 raw  -------->  staged  -------->  fact / analysis  -------->  derived
 data/raw/       data/staged/       data/analysis/              data/derived/ml/
 168 monthly     13,652,322         the fact table, the two     the flight-level
 CSVs, 2.17 GB   flight legs,       city projections and the    modelling table,
 (just fetch)    one parquet per    reconstruction panel        10,200,560 rows
                 year (just stage)  (just fact, just panel)     (just predict-dataset)
                                          ^
                                          |  the article's estimation panel enters
                                          |  data/analysis/ from the side: curated once
                                          |  from the authors' base, not produced by
                                          |  the pipeline (just article-panel)
```

The two panels share the universe, the node map and the carrier sets recorded
in `DECISIONS.md`; the reconstruction follows the article's convention where
the article states one and declares its own where it does not. `data/external/`
holds the hand-curated reference tables those definitions are read from.

## What is regenerated

`data/raw/`, `data/staged/` and `data/derived/` are git-ignored beyond their
`README.md` (and, for `data/raw/`, the manifest). `just fetch && just stage`
rebuilds the raw files (168 files, 2.17 GB) and the staged flight table
(13,652,322 legs, one parquet per year) from ANAC's servers; `just fact` writes
two intermediates under `data/derived/`; `just predict-dataset` writes the
flight-level modelling table under `data/derived/ml/` (10,200,560 rows x 65
columns); `just demo` writes under `data/derived/demo/`. The Zenodo record of
release v1.0.0 archives the repository with its tracked tables; the regenerated
layers are rebuilt from ANAC's files.

## Which panel?

| You want to | Use |
|---|---|
| Re-estimate Tables 2-7 of the article, or start from the authors' final base (the published estimation samples are a subset of it that the recorded filters do not reproduce exactly) | the article's estimation panel, `data/analysis/article_panel_route_month.parquet`: 2002-2013, 24,589 route-months x 52 columns |
| Study delays, concentration or low-cost presence over the full series, or build features for prediction | the reconstruction panel, `data/analysis/panel_route_month.parquet`: 2000-2013, 31,313 route-months x 228 columns |
